💡 BMW Tech Tips
Battery registration is REQUIRED after every BMW battery replacement. Without it the charging system will not manage the new battery correctly and it will fail early.
EPB service mode must be activated via iDrive before touching rear brakes. Forcing pistons without service mode will destroy the EPB motor.
BMW uses DOT 4 Low Viscosity brake fluid only. Standard DOT 4 will trigger ABS fault codes on modern BMW models.
On B58 engines always check VANOS solenoid filter screens before replacing solenoids. Dirty oil clogs screens and mimics solenoid failure.
